# Governance

DoveWAI Protocol is an open technical project stewarded by DoveWAI.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for protocol coherence, compatibility, release decisions, repository security, contribution review, and protecting the project from accidental coupling to private DoveWAI systems.

## Decision model

During the experimental `0.x` phase, DoveWAI maintainers make final merge and release decisions after public technical discussion. Maintainers should prefer interoperable, implementation-neutral proposals with demonstrated demand over vendor-specific convenience.

Major protocol decisions should be recorded in pull requests or design documents so the reasoning remains reviewable.

## Compatibility

New core concepts require evidence that they belong in the shared interoperability layer. Features that are vendor-specific, experimental, or domain-specific should normally begin under a namespaced extension.

## Independence

A conforming implementation must not require a DoveWAI commercial service. DoveWAI may operate hosted or commercial implementations, certification programs, tooling, or services around the protocol without changing the rights granted under the repository license.
