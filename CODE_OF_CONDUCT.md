# Community Conduct

DoveWAI Protocol welcomes technical participation from people with different backgrounds, employers, tools, languages, and implementation choices.

Project discussions must remain respectful, relevant, and focused on technical interoperability. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential sharing, spam, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

Disagreement about protocol design is expected. Critique proposals and evidence rather than people. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive material and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the private process described in `SECURITY.md` rather than demonstrated against other people's systems or disclosed prematurely in public issues.

By participating in this repository, contributors agree to follow this policy in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other official DoveWAI Protocol project spaces.
